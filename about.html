About us

A symbol of peace.
A gesture of thoughtfulness.

The Olive Branch was created from a love for thoughtful gifting — bringing together beautiful objects, meaningful details and the joy of giving. An olive branch has long symbolised peace, connection and a new beginning, which felt like the perfect sentiment for the moments we celebrate and the relationships we cherish.

We believe a gift should feel personal, considered and memorable — not simply like a collection of things in a box. That is what we hope to create with every Olive Branch.

What we believe

The things inside matter.

Most hampers are built to photograph well and then sit in a cupboard. We build them the other way round. The candle is one you will burn, the honey is one you will finish, the journal is one you will actually write in. Nothing is included simply to fill a gap in the box.

We think the best gifts are the ones that become part of someone’s everyday life. Something to enjoy, use, keep or share — chosen because it feels right, not because it simply looks good together.

Where things come from

Made in India, mostly by small makers.

Our candles are hand-poured in India, our honey is raw and unfiltered, and our soaps are cold-pressed and cut by hand. Our gourmet chocolates come from makers in Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu, and wherever possible, we look for products with a story — made by people who care about what goes into them.

Some things are worth keeping. We hope the box becomes one of them — perhaps filled with photographs, letters and memories of its own.
