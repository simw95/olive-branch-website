About us

An olive branch is not a decoration. It is an offering.

We took the name because it says what a gift is for. Peace, thanks, goodwill, an apology — every hamper we compose is doing one of those jobs on someone's behalf.

What we believe

A gift should be used, not displayed.

Most hampers are built to photograph well and then sit in a cupboard. We build them the other way round. The candle is one you will burn, the honey is one you will finish, the journal is one you will actually write in. Nothing is included because it fills a gap in the box.

That principle decides everything else. It is why the boxes are rigid rather than shrink-wrapped, why the food is shelf-stable so it can travel and wait, and why we would rather send five good things than nine forgettable ones.

How we work

Three commitments.

The last box equals the first

Whether an order is for ten or four hundred, every hamper is packed to the same specification. The four hundredth is checked as carefully as the first.

Volume costs less, not more

Our per-hamper price falls in published bands. You will never have to ask for a discount, and we will never quote two customers differently for the same order.

A person, not a portal

Every order is confirmed by someone who has seen it. For gifts that carry someone's name, that matters more than an automated checkout.

Where things come from

Made in India, mostly by small makers.

Our candles are hand poured in India. The honey is raw and unfiltered. The soaps are cold-pressed and cut by hand. Where we buy in — chocolate, tea, olive oil — we buy from producers small enough that we can ask how something was made and get a real answer.

Boxes are rigid board, made to be kept. If a recipient uses ours to store photographs for the next decade, we consider that the gift working exactly as intended.
